Your AI is only as smart as what your data means.

What we build

Six pieces, what the data means.

Modern Data Platform

One governed home for the data that matters.

A lakehouse sized to your decisions, not to every table you own.

  • BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks or Snowflake with open table formats such as Iceberg
  • Streaming and change-data-capture from ERP, POS, CRM and core banking
  • dbt transformation, orchestration and testing
  • Storage and compute designed for cost
Semantic Layer and Metrics

One definition of revenue, margin and churn.

The business vocabulary, written down once and served everywhere.

  • Governed metric store on dbt Semantic Layer, LookML or Cube
  • Certified datasets for BI and for AI
  • Natural-language questions answered from those definitions
  • Access policies by role and region
Enterprise Ontology and Knowledge Graph

A machine-readable model of how your business works.

The map that lets agents reason across systems instead of inside one.

  • Entity and relationship modelling with your domain experts
  • Graph and vector stores together for GraphRAG
  • Entity resolution across systems of record
  • Ontology APIs that agents call as tools
Forecasting and Predictive Models

Numbers about the future that people trust.

Models built for the decision they inform, with uncertainty shown.

  • Demand, cash flow, workforce and capacity forecasting
  • Churn, propensity, credit and risk scoring
  • Hierarchical and probabilistic forecasts with prediction intervals
  • Time-series foundation models benchmarked against classical methods
Decision Intelligence and Optimisation

From a prediction to the recommended action.

Closing the gap between knowing and doing.

  • Optimisation for pricing, inventory, scheduling and routing
  • Scenario and what-if simulation
  • Decision playbooks that trigger an alert or an agent
  • Feedback loops that check whether a recommendation was followed and whether it worked
Data Governance and Quality

Data you can put in front of a regulator.

Controls that run in the pipeline, not in a policy binder.

  • Data contracts and quality observability
  • Lineage and cataloguing on Dataplex, Lake Formation or Unity Catalog
  • PII classification and masking
  • Retention and consent aligned with India’s DPDP Act and Singapore’s PDPA
How we work

A deliverable at the end of every phase.

  1. Decision discovery · Weeks 1–2

    You get Decision backlog with a value estimate per decision

    Rank your top decisions by value; check data readiness for each.

  2. Foundation sprint · Weeks 2–6

    You get Semantic layer v1 and certified datasets

    Ingest, model and certify only the data the first decisions need.

  3. Models in the loop · Weeks 6–12

    You get Production models with backtest results

    Forecasts and ontology live inside the tools people already use.

  4. Scale · Week 12 onward

    You get Monthly decision scorecard

    New domains, retraining cadence, adoption tracking.
